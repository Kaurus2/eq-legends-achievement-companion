## v2026.09.16.4

- Achievement detail panels now show a recent qualifying-kill rate and estimated time remaining for live Slayer counters.
- Estimates begin after five qualifying kills over at least 30 seconds, use up to five minutes of recent activity, and pause after 90 seconds without a qualifying kill.
- A fresh achievement export reconciles confirmed progress and starts a new pace sample. Character changes and log restarts also reset the sample.
- Estimated target completion is distinguished from completion confirmed by the game.
- Fixed percentage text being clipped at the bottom of selected achievement tiles.

The estimate uses kills recognized by the existing matcher, including recognized owned-pet kills. Adding a name to your reference mob list does not enable live matching.

Saved settings, notes and mob-name lists are preserved. Use the in-app updater or download the ZIP for a fresh installation.
