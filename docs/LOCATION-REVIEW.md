# Location review — September 11, 2026

The destination checks support research candidates; they do not establish current spawn availability, Slayer credit or faction safety for every NPC.

- **West Karana:** the map lists bandit camps, brigands, scarecrow fields and the ogre shrine associated with Chief Goonda. The player's no-faction-hit observation is retained only for the targets they fought. [Zone and map](https://eqlwiki.com/Western_Plains_of_Karana)
- **Djinns / Efreetis:** Plane of Sky remains a supported destination. Quest NPCs are not recommended as farm targets. [Plane of Sky](https://eqlwiki.com/Plane_of_Sky)
- **Trooper Kraer:** the location is listed as New Sebilis Expedition. The NPC page says no factions, while the faction page lists a negative hit; this remains a conflict, not a safe camp. [NPC](https://eqlwiki.com/Trooper_Kraer), [faction](https://eqlwiki.com/New_Sebilisian_Expedition)
- **Sphinx targets:** Rathe Mountains has the map landmark, but the NPC table classifies Zazamoukh as Griffin. Counter credit needs an in-game test. [Rathe Mountains](https://eqlwiki.com/Rathe_Mountains)
- **Kerra Island:** distinguish the zone from Erud's Crossing. Older population listings do not establish a safe current race-kill route. [Kerra Island](https://eqlwiki.com/Kerra_Island)
- **Leeches:** the Legends patch notes explicitly replace Rathe Mountains worms with leeches. [May 22 patch](https://eqlwiki.com/Patch_notes_May_22_2026)
- **Hulking gorillas:** listed in Cazic-Thule, including the maze/pyramid area. [Zone NPC table](https://eqlwiki.com/Cazic_Thule)
- **Ratmen:** warriors are listed beyond the first castle in The Hole. [The Hole](https://eqlwiki.com/The_Hole)
- **Reanimated hand:** listed on Unrest's third floor. [Unrest NPC table](https://eqlwiki.com/Estate_of_Unrest)
- **Froglok tads, scarabs, spiders, skeletons, lizardmen, alligators, zombies and spectres:** the Feerrott population list supports these target groups. [The Feerrott](https://eqlwiki.com/The_Feerrott)
- **Runnyeye sporali:** listed, but the page separates historical populations. Current availability still needs checking. [Runnyeye](https://eqlwiki.com/Runnyeye)
- **Other checked population listings:** [Lesser Faydark brownies](https://eqlwiki.com/Lesser_Faydark), [Misty Thicket bixies](https://eqlwiki.com/Misty_Thicket), [Lavastorm elementals, imps and rock dervishes](https://eqlwiki.com/Lavastorm_Mountains), [Steamfont rogue cleaner](https://eqlwiki.com/Steamfont_Mountains), [Greater Faydark wasps](https://eqlwiki.com/Greater_Faydark), [Toxxulia skunks](https://eqlwiki.com/Toxxulia_Forest), [Kedge mermaids](https://eqlwiki.com/Kedge_Keep), [Ocean of Tears gargoyles](https://eqlwiki.com/Ocean_of_Tears), [Fear amygdalans](https://eqlwiki.com/Plane_of_Fear).

The app now includes all-category guidance, 16 class quest contacts, researched faction routes and leads, key guidance, and destinations derived from exploration/Hunter/raid requirements. This does **not** mean every achievement has a complete walkthrough.
