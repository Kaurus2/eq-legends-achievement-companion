- Names added under Mob names now match future personal and recognized pet kills for the selected Slayer achievement. Supports incomplete Slayer achievements with one remaining numeric kill counter.
- Add multiple mob names at once, one per line. Click + or press Ctrl+Enter. Saved names persist, and duplicate names are ignored.
- Slayer tile headers show estimated time remaining, Learning pace, or Paused. Open a tile for the full kills-per-minute breakdown. Estimates learn after five kills over 30 seconds and pause after 90 seconds without qualifying kills.

- Completion popups last 13 seconds and have a pulsing gold border, plus a longer original victory tune. Progress popups remain eight seconds.
- Added the confirmed named dwarf and halfling matches. The crossed-out Keldyn Dunfire is excluded.
- Added a Focus: Banestrike heading above the three rank milestones.
- Reviewed the 12-page App Guide: clickable contents and bookmarks, first-run setup, logging/export instructions, mob-name matching and time estimates.

Live kill counts and time remaining are estimates. A fresh in-game achievement export supplies confirmed progress.

## Updating

Existing users can use Start update inside the app. Saved settings, notes and mob-name lists stay in place. The in-app updater replaces the executable only; download the separate PDF for the revised guide.

The standalone EXE asset is retained for the in-app updater. For a new installation, choose the ZIP package above.
