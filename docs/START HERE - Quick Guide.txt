EQ LEGENDS ACHIEVEMENT COMPANION - START HERE
Current tile-tracker edition

1. Extract the entire ZIP. Run EQ Legends Achievement Companion.exe.
   Keep its data folder beside it in a writable location.

2. Click Configuration. Browse to your character's achievement TXT export,
   then Load / Refresh. Include all categories and completion states when
   exporting from the game if you want a full dashboard.

3. For live tracking, browse to your combat log in the game's Logs folder:
   eqlog_Character_Server.txt. In game, enter /log on.
   Check Enable live updates. Independently choose Show popups + sounds.
   Click Save live settings. Keep the Companion running.

4. Click Configuration again to hide setup. Each tile groups the achievement,
   farming zone, mobs, remaining count and exported progress. Click a tile
   to investigate or edit research. A narrow tracker grows to show details;
   Shrink returns it to its compact width. Resize to one or several columns.

5. Recent progress first promotes matching tiles with a brief animation.
   Show filters opens category, region and zone choices; several selections
   are allowed. Filters still apply to live updates. More includes CSV export,
   research audit and route summary. Table restores the grid for cell copying.
   Right-click a tile to copy its achievement summary.

6. Transparency is in Configuration: enable it and choose 15-100% opacity.
   While using the app it stays fully visible. Switching away applies your chosen opacity.
   15% is faintly visible; 100% is fully visible. Progress can
   briefly restore full opacity before returning to your chosen setting.

7. Sound / popup options offers position, volume and test buttons. Popups
   appear 5% down the primary screen. Completion celebrations offer an original
   short fantasy-style tune and a small fireworks burst. Volume 0 mutes sound.
   Use windowed or borderless game mode for the overlay.

8. Export achievements again periodically. The Companion automatically reads
   the updated file. Live kill counts are estimates; exported counters remain
   the source for the main tracker. Group/raid kills without your personal kill
   message may not be estimated. Old log history is not replayed. Changing only
   popup preferences does not clear estimates.

9. In the detail panel, choose a preferred location and Save research. Add
   alternatives and check evidence before farming. Personal notes do not alter
   tracker values. Research is local and does not sync between guildmates.
   Some locations/faction details remain unverified; see the source-review files.

10. Back up your data folder before upgrading. It will contain your settings,
    personal notes and cached export after use. Share this original ZIP rather
    than your used folder. Do not overwrite your research.json during upgrades.

Windows with .NET Framework 4.x WPF support is required.
