EQ LEGENDS ACHIEVEMENT COMPANION — QUICK GUIDE
Updated September 13, 2026

1. Extract the ZIP to a folder and run EQ Legends Achievement Companion.exe.
2. Open the gear. Choose your EverQuest Legends game folder and click Find characters.
3. Select your character at the top. Export connected and Combat log connected confirm the files were found.
   If the export is missing, export your achievements in game. If the log is missing, use /log on.
   Scan again after creating the files. Advanced file settings contains manual path overrides.
4. Enable live updates to watch new combat log entries. Popups and sounds can be enabled separately.
   Live progress is an estimate. Refresh your achievement export periodically for accurate counters.
5. Click a tile to see locations and research. Click it again to deselect and shrink the panel.
   Farming research is shared; character progress and personal notes are separate.
6. Use Filters to narrow the list, or Table for a copyable grid.
7. The lightbulb switches light/dark mode. Pin keeps the app above other windows.
8. Drag Opacity to preview 15–100% transparency. The percentage changes as you drag.
   Release to save. The app returns to full visibility while focused and uses the saved level when you switch away.
9. Drag unused header space to move the window. Resize its edges. Your position and size are remembered.
10. Sound / popup options includes Test completion to preview the original victory fanfare.

UPGRADING
Close the app, replace only the executable in your existing folder, then reopen it.
Keep your existing data folder: it contains settings, character profiles, research, and notes.
This release has no automatic updater and no DPS meter.
