# Travel sources and limits

Group-port spell names and destinations were checked against the [Legends ritual spell list](https://eqlwiki.com/Rituals), reviewed September 11, 2026. Routes are inferred from the zone connections below. The app compares zone-transition counts among the reviewed regular group ports. It does not measure walking distance or promise the fastest/safest route. Evacuations, private binds, boats, rituals and other transport can offer different choices. A spell being documented does not mean your character has learned it or meets its casting/access requirements.

Some zone pages retain legacy information. Commonlands naming/layout, city access, dungeon entry and planar access still warrant checking in game. Unsupported destinations are explicitly marked as not yet verified; they are not assigned an invented route. Both classes appear in one field and a saved manual route takes precedence over the generated suggestion.
